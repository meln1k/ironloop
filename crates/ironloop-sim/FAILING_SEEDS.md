### Seed 61440: the failsafe landing climbs (`no_flyaway`)

Status: open.

The quad is climbing at about 3.7 m/s toward a 45 m hover when the link drops at 16.4 s. Through `Failsafe(Landing)` from 17.4 s to 24.6 s it keeps climbing at about 2.3 m/s, from 45 m to 62 m, with the throttle swinging between 0.58 and 0.70, until the link returns. Looks like the landing trim and brake keep cycling instead of taking throttle off while the quad is already climbing hard at link loss. Possibly the clearest case of the `failsafe_descends` failures.

### Seed 290248: false `ClockWentBackwards`, then a disarm (`no_false_clock_alarm`)

Status: fixed in the board's control loop (`firmware/mobula6/src/control.rs`) and the simulator's model of it; regression seed.

A 12.6 ms `FcStall` at 47.653 s overlaps an `ImuDropout` that starts 3.5 ms later. At 47.666 s, as the stall ends, the firmware reports `ClockWentBackwards` with no clock fault, and `Disarmed(ImuFailure)` follows 20 ms later. The only one in 1M seeds. Unchecked guess: events queued during the stall are stepped after one stamped later, so the firmware sees a jump back of over 1 ms. If so, either the board can do the same (a firmware bug) or the simulated task queue orders events differently from the board's.

Cause: the last sample before the dropout was ready at 47.656253 s, during the stall. When the stall ended, the loop stepped the ESC telemetry queued during it (stamped up to 47.6655 s) first, as the board's loop does with its `EVENTS` queue, then that sample, 9.8 ms back. Normally a newer data-ready replaces the waiting one every sample, so the queue first is in order; with the IMU silent it is not. On the board, RC frames queued during a long step or stall would do the same. The loop now steps a waiting sample first when it is older than the queued event (`take_drdy_before`, atomic against EXTI1), and the simulator's task does the same. The `Disarmed(ImuFailure)` is right: the dropout lasts 38 ms, and it now comes 20 ms after the last real sample instead of 30.

### Seeds 49056 and 78109: armed upside down, never stopped (`arm_level`, `crash_stops`, `runaway_stops`)

Status: open.

The IMU is mounted flipped (`imu-z-flipped`, `imu-upside-down`) and the quad sits `on-its-side`. It arms at a true tilt of 156° because the estimate says level. 78109 arms and disarms by runaway several times, then stays armed, crashed at 156°, for 4 s, and 5 s after throttle-up. A wrong IMU orientation is a common build mistake. In 78109 all four motors report `MotorFault` right after each arm, which may be a usable signal.

### Seeds 20111 and 45798: runaway detection slow on miswired builds (`runaway_stops`)

Status: open.

20111 (`imu-x-flipped`, `motors-swapped-2-3`): runaway detection fires each time, but one arm stays up 666 ms after throttle-up and reaches 1 m. 45798 (three IMU errors, `motors-swapped-1-4`): 1,151 ms, tumbling until crash detection disarms at 10.8 s. Near the property's limit rather than missed; decide whether the limit or the detector should move.

### Seed 13490: the failsafe hold drops, touchdown not detected (`failsafe_holds`, `failsafe_lands_softly`, `failsafe_disarms_on_touchdown`)

Status: open.

The link drops at 35.3 s right after an inverted hang. In the hold the quad accelerates down at 5.1 m/s², touches down at 3.6 m/s, and sits armed on the ground for over 2 s until the link recovers. Looks like the hover throttle is far off after the maneuver, and touchdown detection misses the hard landing.

### Seed 6763: hardest failsafe touchdown, 4.7 m/s (`failsafe_lands_softly`)

Status: open.

A stuck IMU disarms the quad at 35.9 s; the pilot re-arms in the air at 36.7 s, the link drops 0.3 s later, and the failsafe lands at 4.7 m/s. The failsafe starts with little learned hover throttle after a mid-air re-arm.

### Seed 13292: drifts 120 m on a flapping link (`no_flyaway`)

Status: open.

Wind 4, a bent prop, a critical pack. From 72.8 s to about 85 s the link drops and returns six times, 1–2 s apart, each time `Failsafe(Hold)` → `Landing` → `Recovered`; the quad ends on the ground 120 m from home. Possibly expected (no position hold, strong wind), but each recovery restarts the hold.

### Control quality: 9 seeds (`rate_tracks`, `angle_tracks`, `settles`)

Status: fixed in the monitor (oracle problems, not the controller); 445159, 34731, and 315882 are regression seeds.

- `rate_tracks` 48969, 136715, 143014, 445159: roll rate off by 38–39% RMS; all have `rc-glitch` or `fc-stall` during a stick script.
- `angle_tracks` 133604, 315882, 725433: stick holds reach 69–74% of the commanded angle; 133604 also fails `estimate_tracks`.
- `settles` 34731, 271939: 0.43–0.50 rad/s hands-off roll and pitch; 34731 toggles Angle and Acro several times within 1 s at 13.7 s.

Likely glitched frames or stalls inside the scoring window, so the excuse windows may need adjusting rather than the controller.

Cause, `rate_tracks` and `settles` (6 seeds): in each, one RC frame that passes the CRC (`rc-glitch`) sets a stick near full for one frame, inside a low-amplitude stick hold or the hands-off window. The firmware follows it as far as the motors allow; the first-order reference does not model that, and the 50-150 ms of response carried most of the RMS error. None had `fc-stall` inside the script. The response to a frame off the script is no longer scored for 100 ms after it (`REQ_OFF_SCRIPT_RESPONSE_US`); 0.1 s of off-script sticks still voids the run.

Cause, `angle_tracks`: 315882 starts its script 3.2 s after an impact saturates the gyro, inside the 4 s in which `estimate_tracks` excuses the estimate (17 deg off here); angle mode tilts as far as that estimate says. `angle_tracks` now shares the saturation window (`Cx::reconverging`). 133604 and 725433 also fail `estimate_tracks`: the estimate itself is dragged off 4-5 s after a saturation (see the `estimate_tracks` section).

### `no_esc_stall` (401 in 1M): the weakened motor stalls

Status: fixed in the monitor and the sim (oracle and sim problems); 4285 and 42067 are regression seeds.

All 10 failures in seeds 0-29999 have `motor-degraded`, and in every one the motor that loses sync is the degraded one (60-74% strength), at idle on a pack at 3.0-3.6 V: a weak motor at 10% idle on a sagging pack spins under the ESC's sync limit. The property promises "no ESC loses sync on a sound motor", but the simulated ESC counts any motor still turning as sound, so the monitor counted these. It now judges each motor's stalls only while that motor is healthy. The idle itself holds: with the fix, seeds 0-29999 have no `no_esc_stall` failure, and the "motor idle 5.5%" mutant is still caught.

Seeds 30000-129999 had one more, 42067, on a sound motor: a 190 ms `MotorDesync` on a 3.12 V pack. The simulated ESC stayed `Running` through the fault, so when it cleared it found the motor stopped and, its start ramp capped by that speed, counted a stall 30 ms later. A real ESC that loses sync starts the motor again; the sim's ESC now does too (a sim problem). The restart keeps a desynced motor out for its 50-250 ms open-loop start, so more desyncs tumble the quad: on seeds 30000-129999 `no_esc_stall` now passes everywhere, while `estimate_tracks` goes 211 -> 230, and 22 of the 29 new ones fail 4.0-5.7 s after the gyro saturates, the open post-saturation pull below.

### `motors_quiet` (491 in 1M): a bent prop without eRPM

Status: open, needs a decision (a missing filter, not a bug).

16 of the 17 failures in seeds 0-29999 have both `bent-prop` and `no-telemetry`, and all sit just over the limit (7.5-8.8% RMS). Without eRPM there is no RPM filter, and the 150 Hz gyro low-pass lets the bent prop's shaft-rate tone through to the motors. With telemetry forced on, the same seeds jitter 0.8-1.8%. Betaflight covers this case with its dynamic (FFT) notch. Options: add a dynamic notch, lower the gyro low-pass when eRPM is missing (more lag), or accept it for bent props without bidirectional DShot. The 17th, 7536, is `rc-glitch` corrupting 25% of frames in a 45 m hover (throttle jumps to 100%), the same corrupted-frame issue as the stick scripts above.
